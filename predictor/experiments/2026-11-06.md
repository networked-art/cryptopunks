## Hypotheses and acceptance criteria

Recorded before inspecting development results:

1. Excluding listings with incomplete outcome windows prevents false negative
   labels. Purging overlapping horizons makes holdout scores represent forecasts
   that could actually have been made at the training cutoff.
2. A 365-day half-life for probability training weights adapts to market changes
   better than weighting all historical listings equally.
3. Projecting the three horizon probabilities onto a nondecreasing curve removes
   impossible liquidity forecasts. For nested binary outcomes, this projection
   cannot increase their summed squared error for a fixed set of predictions.

Compare three candidates: mature labels, mature labels with recent weights, and
mature labels with the coherent curve. Select on three chronological 60-day
development folds. Fit only on information available at each fold's origin.
Use a separate subsequent 60-day final fold, ending 30 days before the snapshot
so every outcome is mature. Do not tune again using final results.

Primary score: mean Brier score across the 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day horizons.
Require at least a 1% relative improvement on at least two development folds and
on the final fold, with the final paired daily-block bootstrap interval below
zero. Reject a candidate that worsens any final horizon's Brier score by more
than 5%. Report probability calibration and base-rate comparisons. Keep the
price model unchanged in this cycle, since only ten V2 sales occurred in the
preceding five days. Test full output invariants before release.

```sh
uv run python scripts/evaluate_probability.py .evaluation/2026-11-06.pkl.gz --phase development --output experiments/2026-11-06-development.json
```

The snapshot hash and timestamp are recorded in each JSON report. Future cycles
must capture new snapshots and advance the evaluation windows. Do not treat this
cycle's final fold as unseen data again.

## Development decision

Selected the mature-label model with the coherent probability curve before
opening the reserved final fold. Its mean Brier score improved by 1.01% and
1.62% in the first two development folds, and regressed by 0.78% in the third.
The recency-weighted candidate was worse in two folds and is rejected.
The selected candidate has no reversed horizon probabilities in any fold.

Final command, using only the selected candidate:

```sh
uv run python scripts/evaluate_probability.py .evaluation/2026-11-06.pkl.gz --phase final --candidate coherent --output experiments/2026-11-06-final.json
```

## Final result: reject the candidate

The reserved final fold contains 830 listings from August 8 through October 7,
2026. The snapshot cutoff is November 6 at 16:25:01 UTC.

| Metric | Incumbent | Candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Mean Brier across horizons | 0.154805 | 0.153876 |
| 24-hour Brier | 0.118302 | 0.118681 |
| 7-day Brier | 0.187995 | 0.191019 |
| 30-day Brier | 0.158118 | 0.151929 |
| Reversed horizon curves | 6 | 0 |
| Near-floor mean Brier, 541 listings | 0.189385 | 0.187782 |
| Premium mean Brier, 289 listings | 0.090073 | 0.090405 |

The 0.60% aggregate improvement misses the 1% minimum. The paired daily-block
bootstrap 95% interval for candidate minus incumbent Brier is
[-0.002682, +0.000744], so the result does not establish an improvement.
The 30-day calibration error improved, but the 24-hour score and calibration
error worsened slightly. The candidate is rejected. Do not deploy its training
or inference changes or retune against this final fold.

This cycle adds evaluation tools, reports, and listing identity/outcome metadata.
The metadata is excluded from the model's explicit feature column list. The
production training and inference logic remain unchanged, and no new model is
deployed. Future cycles can revisit the correctness issues using new evidence.

## Observed live price outcomes

Archived served prices were matched to subsequent sales using the latest
promoted run completed before each sale. Actual activation timestamps were not
recorded. Exclude sales within one minute of training completion rather than
claiming exact publication timing. No matched sale in this snapshot fell in that guard.

| Standard and window | Sales | Median absolute percentage error | Value-weighted error | p10-p90 coverage |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| V2, 5 days | 10 | 4.65% | 7.60% | 80.0% |
| V2, 14 days | 42 | 6.99% | 8.88% | 71.4% |
| V2, 90 days | 308 | 5.72% | 10.90% | 76.9% |
| V1, 90 days | 10 | 158.70% | 139.98% | 20.0% |

There were no V1 sales in the five-day or 14-day windows. The V1 discrepancy
needs investigation, but ten sales do not support selecting and validating a
new multiplier in this cycle. V2 interval coverage also merits further work.

Probability evaluation concerns observed listing asks. It does not establish
calibration for hypothetical quick-sale asks or V1's probability heuristic.
Price results describe the existing model, not improvements made in this cycle.
