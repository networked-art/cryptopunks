forecasts and zero missing predictions or data-quality exclusions. Publication

query: one forecast is from 0.1.1 and eight from 0.1.2. The eight post-release

Both interval misses use the November 25 run: Punk #5206 sold below its lower
bound (26.43 ETH versus 28.23 ETH) and #2290 sold above its upper bound (42 ETH
versus 37.12 ETH). Opposite errors in the same run do not support a uniform
price shift. The current indexer is fresh and forecast ages are under 24 hours;
no access or indexer-staleness failure was observed. This review does not isolate
market movement from individual-Punk model error, so it does not justify a
specific fitted correction. The 14-day V2 window has 32 sales, 6.74% median
error, 9.70% value-weighted error and 78.13% coverage. The 90-day window has
293 V2 sales and 74.06% coverage; that persistent undercoverage remains a useful
